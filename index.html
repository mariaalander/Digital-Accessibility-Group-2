Protect Yourself Online

Accessible cyber safety information for every citizen.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing: fake messages asking for personal or bank details.
  • Scams: offers that create urgency, fear, or promise easy money.
  • Malware: harmful software hidden in links, files, or apps.

How to Protect Yourself

  1. Use strong, unique passwords for each account.
  2. Turn on multi-factor authentication when possible.
  3. Check website addresses and sender details before clicking links.
  4. Never share one-time passcodes or banking PINs.
  5. Keep your phone, computer, and apps updated.

If You Suspect Fraud

  1. Stop contact with the suspected scammer immediately.
  2. Contact your bank to secure your account.
  3. Change compromised passwords right away.
  4. Report the incident to your local cybercrime or police service.
